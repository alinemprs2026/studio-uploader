Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Studio Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app accesses

When a channel owner connects a channel with Google Sign-In, the app receives an access token for these permissions only:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos and thumbnails to that channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the channel's ID, title and its list of videos.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read view and watch-time statistics for the owner's private dashboard.

The app does not access Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

  • Tokens and statistics are stored on the owner's private server and used only to upload the owner's videos and display the owner's statistics.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Third-party apps with account access). When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and the related data is deleted from the server within 30 days.

Contact

For questions, use the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen of this app.
