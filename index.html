Studio Uploader

Studio Uploader is a private, internal tool used by its owner to publish videos and thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channels, on a schedule, using the official YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to the public. Only channels that belong to the owner are connected, and each channel is connected only after the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission.

What it does

  • Uploads videos and custom thumbnails to the connected channel.
  • Reads basic channel and video statistics (views, watch time) to show them in the owner's private dashboard.
